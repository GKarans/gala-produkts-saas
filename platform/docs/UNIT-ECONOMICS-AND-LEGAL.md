# Unit economics and legal release gates

Run `npm run cost -- 100` and `npm run cost -- 10000`. The editable model uses the approved EUR prices, a 20/60/20 Single/Gathering/Studio mix, 55% allowance use, 1.08 MiB per optimized photo plus thumbnail, plan retention periods, and explicit R2/payment assumptions. It intentionally excludes VAT, corporate tax, refunds, chargebacks, support, email, monitoring and hosting; the output is contribution margin, not profit.

Stripe Checkout collects billing addresses and tax IDs and requests automatic tax calculation. Before sales, a Latvian accountant or lawyer must approve seller identity, VAT registration/treatment, invoice requirements, B2B reverse-charge handling, consumer withdrawal and digital-content consent, refund language, retention/erasure terms, processor agreements, privacy notice, cookie position, and incident process.

A paid pilot is evidence, not code: recruit at least 10 representative organizers, charge the real published price, record conversion, event/photo usage, support time, refunds and willingness to buy again. No pricing claim is considered validated until the pilot is completed and reviewed.
