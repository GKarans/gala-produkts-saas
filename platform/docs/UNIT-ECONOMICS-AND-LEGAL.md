# Unit economics and legal release gates

Run `npm run cost -- 100` and `npm run cost -- 10000`. The editable model uses the approved EUR prices, a 20/60/20 Single/Gathering/Studio mix, 55% allowance use, 1.08 MiB per optimized photo plus thumbnail, plan retention periods, and explicit R2/payment assumptions. It intentionally excludes VAT, corporate tax, refunds, chargebacks, support, email, monitoring and hosting; the output is contribution margin, not profit.

The local checkout is a simulation: it does not collect payment details, charge money, issue invoices, or connect to a payment provider. Klix and Swedbank are only candidates; no provider, merchant account, tax calculation, or invoice flow has been selected or integrated. Do not treat draft prices or this cost model as approved customer-facing commercial terms.

Before sales, complete the seller and contact details after registration, confirm the VID/e-structure and VAT position with an accountant, and have a Latvian lawyer review distance-selling disclosures, digital-service commencement/withdrawal flow, refunds, event-photo sharing, retention/deletion, privacy roles and processor agreements. Verify a selected provider's merchant eligibility, fees, settlement, signed payment notifications, receipts/invoices, and reconciliation before enabling real checkout. Company, registration and domain fields intentionally remain blank until decided.

A paid pilot is evidence, not code: recruit at least 10 representative organizers, charge the real published price, record conversion, event/photo usage, support time, refunds and willingness to buy again. No pricing claim is considered validated until the pilot is completed and reviewed.
